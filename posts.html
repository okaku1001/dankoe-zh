网红（influencer）受注意力驱动，所以他们的内容必须被优化成“消费完就忘”（廉价多巴胺）。

个人品牌受金钱驱动，所以他们的内容必须被优化成把你一步步推进他们的销售漏斗。

匠人（artisan）受作品质量带来的满足感驱动。他们需要注意力和金钱，但唯一的目的是把更多时间花在自己热爱的工作及其影响上。

成为一名匠人。

EN 原文

An influencer is incentivized by attention, so their content must be optimized to be consumed and forgotten (cheap dopamine).

A personal brand is incentivized by money, so their content must be optimized to move you down their sales funnel.

An artisan is incentivized by the satisfaction that comes from the quality of their work. They need attention and money, but for the sole purpose of spending more time on work they enjoy and the impact it has.

Become an artisan.
